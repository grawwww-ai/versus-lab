One billet of titanium.

Rims, bridge and hinges, machined from a single piece of Grade-5 titanium and trimmed to 24 grams. You notice it in the first minute — and never again.

Sound with a direction.

A beam-array in each temple aims audio into your ear canal and nowhere else. To the person beside you, your conversation is a whisper of nothing.

Six ears, always open.

Six microphones form a beam toward whoever is speaking — and ignore the rest. From the second they start talking, the translation is already underway.

Capabilities

Technology that gets out of the way.

No screen to stare at. Nothing to unlock, pair or charge mid-sentence. Halo simply sits in the conversation, translating.

Fluent, both ways

Halo listens to the person in front of you, translates in 0.28 s, and whispers it into your ear in a voice that sounds like yours. When you answer, it speaks for you — out loud, in their language.

Private by physics

Sound is focused into your ear canal — no seal, no earbud, no leak. The person right next to you hears silence. Privacy here isn't a setting; it's geometry.

Six microphones, one voice

A beamforming array finds the person speaking and follows them — through wind, a busy café, or a train platform at rush hour.

Fluent when the signal isn't

Each temple carries its own neural engine, translating on-device. Airplane mode, basements, mountain trails — the conversation doesn't notice.

43 languages · with Halo Cloud

Neural engine: 2 × 11 TOPS, one in each temple. Flip the switch to leave the network behind.

And when nobody's talking, Halo is simply a lovely pair of headphones — music, calls and podcasts for 40 hours with the case.
